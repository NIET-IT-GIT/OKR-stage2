Member Portal

Track your Key Results and respond to periodic check-in requests sent by your admin team.

Getting Started

Overview Member

The Member Portal is where all staff track their individual Key Results (KRs) and respond to periodic check-in requests. Your manager and admin team can see your submissions and progress rate.

ℹ
Your roleYou have a Member account if you are a general staff member. Managers also see this portal for their own personal KRs alongside their Manager Portal.
PageWhat you do here
DashboardSee your overall KR completion rate and current KR targets
My Check-insSubmit responses to check-in requests sent by your admin
ReportsRead company-wide OKR reports published by Admin
Dashboard

My Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a snapshot of your current OKR performance. Your completion rate is shown as a percentage with a colour-coded status.

Completion Rate
e.g. 84.2% — average across all active KRs that have received check-ins
Status
On Track At Risk Off Track — based on completion vs. target pace
Pending Check-ins
Badge count showing unanswered check-in requests waiting for you

Understanding your completion rate

Your rate is calculated only from KRs that have had a check-in sent to you. KRs with no check-in ever sent do not affect your rate. Once a check-in is sent and goes unanswered for more than 24 hours, that KR is counted as 0% until you respond and your manager approves the submission.

⚠
24-hour ruleIf you receive a check-in email and do not respond within 24 hours, that KR will show as 0% in your rate and in your department's rate. Always respond promptly.
Check-ins

My Check-ins Page

When your admin sends a check-in, you receive an email and a card appears here. Each card represents one Key Result for a specific review period.

Each card shows:

  • KR name — what is being measured
  • Target — the number you were expected to reach
  • Review period — the time window this check-in covers (always the previous period)
ℹ
Review period is the past periodCheck-ins always cover the period that just ended. A weekly check-in sent on Monday covers last week's work (Mon–Sun), not the current week.
Responding to Check-ins

Submitting "Yes" — Target Met

Click ✓ Yes when you have met or exceeded the target for the review period.

  1. 1
    Click ✓ YesYour response is recorded immediately. The system sets your actual value equal to the target.
  2. 2
    Submission goes to your managerThe card shows "Pending approval" until your manager reviews it.
  3. 3
    Manager approvesYour KR completion rate updates to reflect the approved actual value.
✓
TipYour rate does not update until approval. If your manager has not approved within a few days, follow up with them directly.
Responding to Check-ins

Submitting "No" — Target Not Met

Click ✗ No if you did not fully meet the target. You will be asked to provide a reason and your actual value.

  1. 1
    Click ✗ NoAn inline form opens below the card.
  2. 2
    Enter your reasonExplain briefly why the target was not met. Be specific — this goes to your manager.
  3. 3
    Enter your actual valueType the number you actually achieved (e.g. 38 out of a target of 50).
  4. 4
    Click Submit NoYour submission is sent to your manager for review, including the reason and actual value you entered.
⚠
Actual value mattersThe number you enter here directly determines your KR completion rate once approved. Enter the real figure — your manager may adjust it if they disagree.
History

Submission History

Answered check-ins appear below the pending section. Each answered card shows your response, the review period, and the approval status.

StatusMeaning
Pending approvalYou have answered; waiting for your manager to approve or reject
ApprovedManager approved — your KR actual value has been updated and counts toward your rate
RejectedManager disagreed and has set a different actual value; your KR is updated with their figure
Reports

Company Reports

The Reports page shows company-wide OKR reports published by the Admin team. Reports are permanent snapshots — they do not change after publishing.

Each report shows:

  • Department Rankings — KR completion rates by department at time of publishing
  • Top Performers — highest-achieving staff members that period
  • Needs Improvement — staff flagged as below target that period
  • Submission Rate — what percentage of check-ins received a "Yes" answer
ℹ
Reports are read-only for Members and Managers. Only Admins can publish, edit, or delete reports.

Approvals

Approving a Submission

When a team member submits a check-in (either Yes or No), it appears in your Approve Submissions queue.

  1. 1
    Open Approve SubmissionsA badge on the nav item shows how many submissions are awaiting your review.
  2. 2
    Review the submission cardEach card shows: KR name, target, review period, the member's answer (Yes/No), their reported actual value, and (for No answers) their reason.
  3. 3
    Click ApproveThe member's KR actual value is set to what they reported. Their completion rate updates immediately.
Approvals

Rejecting a Submission

Reject a submission when you believe the member's reported actual value is incorrect. You must provide the correct actual value when rejecting.

  1. 1
    Click RejectAn inline form opens on the submission card.
  2. 2
    Enter the correct actual valueType the number you believe accurately reflects the member's performance for that period.
  3. 3
    Confirm rejectionThe member's KR actual value is set to your entered value, not theirs.
⚠
Rejection is definitiveA rejected submission is final. The member's KR actual is immediately updated with the value you provided. There is no further appeal step in the system.

ViewKR types includedSubmission window

How department rates are calculated

Each department's rate is the average of its members' individual completion rates. A member is included only if they have at least one answered or overdue check-in in the selected window. Members with no check-ins sent are excluded so early-period gaps do not drag a department's rate down unfairly.

ℹ
No data yet?If no check-ins have been sent in the selected window, a "No check-in submissions for this period yet" message appears. Switch to Annual Overview to see all-time data, or send check-ins first.
Configuration

Departments & KR Configuration

The Departments page is where you set up the KR structure that staff are measured against. Each department contains:

  • Department-level KRs — shared by all members of the department
  • Teams — sub-groups within the department, each with their own KRs
  • Member KRs — personal KRs assigned to individual staff members

Adding a KR

  1. 1
    Select the departmentUse the sidebar or the Departments nav item to open a department.
  2. 2
    Click Add KRIn the appropriate section (dept-level, team, or member).
  3. 3
    Fill in KR detailsLabel, target value, unit, operator (≥ or ≤), and period type.
  4. 4
    SaveChanges sync to the database automatically.

KR Fields

Label
Description of the key result, e.g. "Complete 50 training hours"
Target
The number to reach. For monthly KRs you can set a different target per month.
Unit
Optional unit shown in emails and cards, e.g. "hours", "%", "calls"
Operator
≥ means higher is better; ≤ means lower is better (e.g. error rate)
Period
How often this KR is checked: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, bi-annual, or annual

Operations

Sending Check-in Emails

Check-in emails trigger the entire submission-and-approval workflow. Each send creates submission records for all relevant staff and dispatches emails.

  1. 1
    Select the period typeChoose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, or Annual. Only staff with KRs of that type will receive check-ins.
  2. 2
    Optionally select a specific userLeave blank to send to all eligible staff, or pick one individual for a targeted re-send.
  3. 3
    Click Send Check-inEmails are dispatched and submission records are created. A confirmation banner shows how many submissions were created.
ℹ
Duplicate preventionIf you send the same period type twice in the same window, only staff who did not receive a submission the first time will get one. Already-sent check-ins are not duplicated.
⚠
Check-ins cover the previous periodA weekly check-in sent today asks staff to review last week, not this week. The email subject and the submission card both show the exact review date range.

The 24-hour rule

If a staff member does not respond within 24 hours of a check-in being sent, that KR is automatically counted as 0% in all rate calculations. The rate is corrected as soon as the member responds and the submission is approved.

Reporting

OKR Reports

Published reports are permanent snapshots of performance at a point in time. They are visible to all roles but only Admins can create, edit, or delete them.

⚠
Reports are frozen snapshotsOnce published, the data inside a report does not update even if more check-ins are approved afterward. Publish when you are satisfied the data is complete for the period.

Publishing the current month's report

  1. 1
    Review the Current Data Preview bannerThis live panel shows what will be published: submission rate, top performers, and staff needing attention.
  2. 2
    Click "Publish [Month] Report"A snapshot is taken immediately and stored. The report appears in the list for all roles.

Generating a report for a custom period

  1. 1
    Click "Generate for Period"An input panel expands above the report list.
  2. 2
    Enter a Period LabelThis is the display name, e.g. "Q1 FY2026" or "Week 22".
  3. 3
    Enter From and To datesOnly check-in submissions with a send date in this range are counted.
  4. 4
    Click "Generate & Publish"The report is created and immediately visible to all roles.
