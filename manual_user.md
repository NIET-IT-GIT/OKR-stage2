# NIET OKR Platform — Staff User Manual

## Overview

As a staff member, you use the NIET OKR Platform to track your personal KPI progress, submit weekly work outcomes to your manager, and view your submission history and company-wide monthly reports.

---

## Logging In

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Click **Sign in with Microsoft** to authenticate with your NIET account.
3. Once authenticated, you will land on the **My KPIs** page.

---

## Navigation

The sidebar on the left contains all pages available to you:

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| My KPIs | View your personal KPI progress |
| Weekly Submission | Update KPI actuals and submit your weekly outcomes |
| My History | View all your past submissions and their status |
| Monthly Reports | View company-wide published reports |

---

## My KPIs

This page shows your personal KPI performance at a glance.

### Summary Metrics

At the top of the page you will see:
- **Completion %** — your overall KPI completion rate relative to the target pace.
- **KRs tracked** — the number of key results you are measured on.
- **Weekly submission** — whether you have submitted this week (shows "Submitted" or "Due").
- **Pending review** — how many of your past submissions are awaiting manager review.

### Key Result Cards

Each KR you are tracked on is displayed as a card showing:
- The KR ID (in monospace font)
- The KR description
- Your current actual value (displayed large, colour-coded by status)
- The target value
- A large progress bar
- Your completion % for that KR
- A status tag: **On Track** (green), **At Risk** (yellow), or **Behind** (red)

---

## Weekly Submission

Use this page each week to update your KPI actuals and report your work outcomes to your manager.

### Updating KPI Actuals

The form shows each of your key results with:
- The KR label and target (read-only)
- An **actual** field you can update
- Auto-calculated completion % and progress bar

Update your actuals to reflect your current progress before submitting.

### Reporting Work Outcomes

In the **Work Outcomes** section:
- The **Week** field is pre-filled with the current week label (you can edit it if needed).
- In the text area, write a summary of what you worked on this week — tasks completed, wins, and any blockers.

### Submitting

Click **Submit for Approval** to send your update to your manager. Your manager will review and mark it as Approved or Rejected.

You can submit multiple times; each submission is recorded separately in your history.

---

## My History

This page shows all of your past weekly submissions.

Submissions are listed newest first. Each card shows:
- The week label
- The date you submitted
- Your work outcome items (full text)
- The approval status: **Pending** (awaiting review), **Approved**, or **Rejected**
- Any notes your manager added when reviewing

If you have not submitted anything yet, the page will show: *"No submissions yet — go to Weekly Submission to log your first one."*

You cannot edit or delete past submissions from this page.

---

## Monthly Reports

This page shows company-wide performance reports published by the Admin. Reports are visible to all staff.

Each report shows:
- The report period (e.g. May 2026 or Q1 FY2026)
- The published date
- Company-wide completion rate
- **Department Rankings** — departments ranked #1 to #N by completion rate
- **Top Performers** — the top 3 staff members with their completion rates
- **Needs Improvement** — staff flagged as below the performance threshold (action required)

You cannot create or delete reports.

---

## Signing Out

Click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the sidebar. This clears your session and returns you to the login screen.
