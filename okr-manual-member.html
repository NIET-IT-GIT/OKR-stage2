Getting Started

Overview

The Member Portal is where you track your individual Key Results (KRs) and respond to periodic check-in requests. Your manager and the admin team can see your submissions and your overall progress rate.

ℹ
Who sees this guide?This guide is for general staff members. If you are a Manager, you also have access to this portal for your own personal KRs — this guide covers that part of your experience too.
PageWhat you do here
DashboardSee your overall KR completion rate and current KR targets at a glance
My Check-InsSubmit responses to check-in requests sent by your admin
ReportsRead company-wide OKR reports published by the Admin team
Access

Signing In

The portal is accessed through your NIET Group Microsoft account — the same credentials you use for Outlook and Office 365. No separate password is required.

  1. 1
    Open the portal in your browserNavigate to the OKR portal address provided by your admin. The landing page shows a single sign-in button.
  2. 2
    Click "Sign in with Microsoft"You will be redirected to the Microsoft login page.
  3. 3
    Enter your NIET Group credentialsUse your NIET Group email address (e.g. firstname.lastname@niet.edu.au) and your usual Microsoft password.
  4. 4
    You are signed inMicrosoft redirects you back to the portal and you land on your dashboard automatically.
✓
Already signed in to Microsoft?If you are already logged in to your NIET Group Microsoft account on the device, clicking "Sign in with Microsoft" will authenticate you instantly without prompting for a password.
⚠
Sign in issues?If you see an error after signing in, your account may not have been set up in the portal yet. Contact your admin to confirm your account exists and is assigned to the correct department.
Dashboard

My Dashboard

The dashboard gives you a snapshot of your current OKR performance. Your completion rate is shown as a percentage with a colour-coded status indicator.

Completion Rate
e.g. 84.2% — your average across all active KRs that have had a check-in sent
Status
On Track At Risk Off Track — based on your rate vs. target pace
Pending Check-Ins
Badge count showing how many unanswered check-ins are waiting for your response

How your completion rate is calculated

Your rate is calculated only from KRs that have had a check-in sent to you. KRs that have never received a check-in do not affect your rate at all.

For each KR with a check-in sent, your completion is: actual value ÷ target value. Your overall rate is the average across all such KRs.

If a check-in goes unanswered for more than 24 hours, that KR is automatically counted as 0% until you respond and your manager approves. See the 24-Hour Rule section below.

Check-Ins

My Check-Ins Page

When your admin sends a check-in, you receive an email and a card appears on this page. Each card represents one of your Key Results for a specific review period.

Each card shows:

  • KR name — what is being measured
  • Target — the number you were expected to reach for that period
  • Review period — the exact date range this check-in covers
  • Yes / No buttons — your response options
ℹ
Review period is always the pastCheck-Ins always cover the period that just ended, not the current one. A weekly check-in sent on Monday covers last week (Mon–Sun). The email and card both show the exact date range so there is no ambiguity.
Responding to Check-Ins

Submitting "Yes" — Target Met

Click ✓ Yes when you have met or exceeded the target for the review period.

  1. 1
    Click ✓ Yes on the check-in cardYour response is recorded immediately. The system treats your actual value as equal to the target.
  2. 2
    The card moves to "Pending approval"Your manager will receive the submission for review. No further action is needed from you.
  3. 3
    Manager approvesYour KR completion rate updates to reflect the approved actual value.
✓
Rate doesn't update until approvalYour rate will not change until your manager approves the submission. If several days pass without approval, follow up with your manager directly.
Responding to Check-Ins

Submitting "No" — Target Not Met

Click ✗ No if you did not fully meet the target for the review period. You will be asked to provide a brief reason and the actual value you achieved.

  1. 1
    Click ✗ No on the check-in cardAn inline form opens directly below the card — you do not leave the page.
  2. 2
    Enter your reasonBriefly explain why the target was not met. Be specific — this reason is visible to your manager and helps them make a fair decision.
  3. 3
    Enter your actual valueType the number you actually achieved, e.g. 38 out of a target of 50.
  4. 4
    Click Submit NoYour submission is sent to your manager, including the reason and actual value you entered.
⚠
Enter the real figureThe actual value you enter directly determines your KR completion rate once approved. Your manager may adjust it if they believe it is inaccurate, so always enter the genuine number.

Important Rule

The 24-Hour Rule

When a check-in is sent to you, you have 24 hours to respond. If you do not respond within that window:

  • That KR is automatically counted as 0% completion in your rate
  • Your department's rate is also affected
  • The 0% is corrected as soon as you respond and your manager approves
⚠
Check your email promptlyCheck-In emails come from the NIET Group OKR system. If you are expecting a check-in and haven't received an email, check your junk/spam folder and contact your admin if it is not there.
Reports

Company Reports

The Reports page shows company-wide OKR reports published by the Admin team. Each report is a permanent snapshot of performance at the time of publishing — the figures do not change after a report is published.

Each report shows:

  • Company Rate — overall completion rate across all departments
  • Department Rankings — each department's KR completion rate at time of publishing
  • Top Performers — highest-achieving staff members for that period
  • Action Required — staff who were below target pace for the period
  • Submission Rate — what percentage of check-ins received a "Yes" answer
ℹ
Reports are read-only for Members and Managers. Only Admins can publish, edit, or delete them.
