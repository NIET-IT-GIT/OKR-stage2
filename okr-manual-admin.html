Getting Started

Overview

The Admin Portal gives you full control over the OKR system. Admins are the only role that can create or modify data — all other roles are read-only or limited to their own submissions.

Access

Signing In

The portal is accessed through your NIET Group Microsoft account — the same credentials you use for Outlook and Office 365. No separate password is required.

  1. 1
    Open the portal in your browserNavigate to the OKR portal address. The landing page shows a single sign-in button.
  2. 2
    Click "Sign in with Microsoft"You will be redirected to the Microsoft login page.
  3. 3
    Enter your NIET Group credentialsUse your NIET Group email address (e.g. firstname.lastname@niet.edu.au) and your usual Microsoft password.
  4. 4
    You are signed inMicrosoft redirects you back to the portal and you land on the Admin Portal dashboard automatically.
✓
Already signed in to Microsoft?If you are already logged in to your NIET Group Microsoft account on the device, clicking "Sign in with Microsoft" will authenticate you instantly without prompting for a password.
ℹ
First-time setupBefore other staff can sign in, you must create their accounts in the Users page and assign them the correct role and department. Users who sign in before their account is created will see an access error.

How department completion rates are calculated

Each department's rate is the average of its members' individual KR completion rates. A member is included in the average only if they have at least one answered or overdue check-in within the selected window. Members with no check-ins sent in the window are excluded entirely, so early-period gaps do not drag a department's rate down unfairly.

ℹ
No data messageIf no check-ins have been sent in the selected window, the dashboard shows "No check-in submissions for this period yet." Switch to Annual Overview to see all available data, or send check-ins first.
Configuration

Departments & KR Configuration

The Departments page is where you build the KR structure that all staff are measured against. Each department has three levels of KRs:

  • Department-level KRs — apply to every member of the department
  • Team KRs — apply to all members of a specific team within the department
  • Member KRs — personal KRs assigned to a specific individual

Adding a new KR

  1. 1
    Open the departmentSelect it from the sidebar or the Departments nav item.
  2. 2
    Click Add KR in the appropriate sectionDepartment-level, a specific team, or under a specific member.
  3. 3
    Fill in the KR fieldsLabel, target, unit, operator, and period type (see field descriptions below).
  4. 4
    SaveThe KR is active immediately and will be included in the next check-in send for its period type.

KR Fields

Label
Description shown in emails and cards, e.g. "Complete 50 training hours"
Target
The number the member must reach. Monthly KRs support a different target per month.
Unit
Optional label shown after the number, e.g. "hours", "%", "calls"
Operator
≥ means higher is better; ≤ means lower is better (e.g. complaint rate, error count)
Period Type
How often this KR is checked: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, bi-annual, or annual

Operations

Sending Check-in Emails

Sending a check-in is what triggers the entire submission-and-approval workflow. It creates submission records for all eligible staff and dispatches personalised emails to each of them.

  1. 1
    Select the period typeChoose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annual, or Annual. Only staff who have KRs of that type will receive check-ins.
  2. 2
    Optionally select a specific userLeave blank to send to all eligible staff. Select a user for a targeted re-send to one individual.
  3. 3
    Click Send Check-inEmails are dispatched and submission records are created. A confirmation banner shows how many submissions were created.
ℹ
Duplicate preventionIf you send the same period type twice in the same period window, only staff who did not receive a submission the first time will get one. Existing check-ins are never duplicated.
⚠
Check-ins always cover the previous periodA weekly check-in sent today asks staff to review last week, not the current week. Both the email and the submission card show the exact date range so staff know which period they are reporting on.

The 24-hour rule

If a staff member does not respond within 24 hours of a check-in being sent, that KR is automatically counted as 0% in all rate calculations — including their individual rate, their department's rate, and the Company Overview. The rate corrects itself as soon as the member responds and their manager approves.

Reporting

OKR Reports

Published reports are permanent snapshots of organisational performance at a point in time. All roles (Admin, Manager, Member) can read them, but only Admins can create, edit, or delete them.

⚠
Reports are frozen at publish timeOnce published, the data in a report does not update — even if more check-ins are approved afterward. Publish only when you are satisfied the period's data is complete.

Publishing the current month's report

  1. 1
    Review the Current Data PreviewThis live banner shows what will be captured: company rate, top performers, submission rate, and staff needing attention.
  2. 2
    Click "Publish [Month] Report"A snapshot is taken immediately and the report is visible to all roles straight away.

Generating a report for a custom period

  1. 1
    Click "Generate for Period"An input panel expands above the report list.
  2. 2
    Enter a Period LabelThe display name shown in the report card, e.g. "Q1 FY2026" or "Week 22".
  3. 3
    Enter From and To datesOnly check-in submissions with a send date within this range will be included in the stats.
  4. 4
    Click "Generate & Publish"The report is created and immediately visible to all roles.

Editing a published report

Click Edit on any report card to update its Period Label or add Notes. The underlying data (rates, rankings) cannot be changed after publishing.

Configuration

Email Templates

Customise the check-in email for each period type. Each period type (Weekly, Monthly, etc.) has its own independent template. Changes take effect on the next send.

PlaceholderReplaced with when email is sent
{periodLabel}The period type label, e.g. "Monthly"
{periodLower}Lowercase label, e.g. "monthly"
{periodKey}The specific period key, e.g. "2026-06" for June 2026

Template fields

From Name
Sender display name shown in the recipient's email client (default: "NIET Group OKR")
Subject
Email subject line. Use placeholders to include the period dynamically.
Body
Opening paragraph shown above the KR table in the email.
CTA Text
Label on the submit button in the email, e.g. "Submit My Check-in →"
Footer
Small-print text at the bottom of the email.
✓
Always send a test before a bulk sendUse the "Send Test Email" button on the Email Templates page to preview exactly how the email renders before dispatching it to all staff.
