# NIET OKR Platform — Manager User Manual

## Overview

As a Manager, you oversee your department's key results, approve weekly submissions from your direct reports, manage team projects, and update KPI actuals. You also have read access to company-wide monthly reports.

---

## Logging In

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Click **Sign in with Microsoft** to authenticate with your NIET account.
3. Once authenticated, you will land on the **Team Dashboard**.

---

## Navigation

The sidebar on the left contains all pages available to you:

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Team Dashboard | Overview of your team's performance |
| Department KPIs | View and update department and team key results |
| Approve Submissions | Review and approve/reject member weekly submissions |
| Projects | Create and track your team projects |
| Edit Member KPIs | Update KPI actuals for individual team members |
| Monthly Reports | View company-wide published reports |

A badge on **Approve Submissions** shows how many submissions are currently pending your review.

---

## Team Dashboard

Your dashboard gives a quick snapshot of how your team is performing:

- **Department completion rate** — your department's aggregated KPI progress.
- **Direct reports** — the number of staff reporting to you.
- **Pending approvals** — how many weekly submissions are awaiting your review.

Below the summary, each direct report is listed with:
- Their avatar, name, and job title
- Their most recent weekly submission (week label and status)
- Their overall completion rate and progress bar
- A status tag: **On Track** (green), **At Risk** (yellow), or **Behind** (red)

---

## Department KPIs

This page shows the key results for your department and the teams you manage.

### Department-Level KRs

The top section shows your department's key results with:
- KR ID, label, target, actual, unit, and data source
- Completion % and progress bar for each KR
- Overall department completion rate

To update a KR's actual value, click into the actual field, enter the new value, and save.

### Team-Level KRs

Below the department KRs, each team you manage is shown with its own set of KRs. You can view and update actuals for team KRs the same way as department KRs.

---

## Approve Submissions

This page lists all weekly submissions from your direct reports.

### Reviewing a Submission

Each submission card shows:
- Member avatar, name, and job title
- The week and submission date
- A description of their work outcomes for that week
- The current status (Pending / Approved / Rejected)

Submissions are sorted with **Pending** first, then by date (newest to oldest).

### Approving or Rejecting

For any submission with a **Pending** status:
- Click **Approve** to mark it as approved.
- Click **Reject** to mark it as rejected.

Once a submission has been reviewed, its status updates immediately and is visible to the member in their history.

---

## Projects

Use this page to create and manage your team's projects.

### Creating a Project

1. Enter a project name in the **Project name...** field.
2. Select a due date.
3. Click the create button. The project is created with 0% completion and Active status.

### Viewing Projects

Each project card shows: name, due date, last updated timestamp, completion %, progress bar, status (Active/Completed), and any log notes.

Summary metrics at the top show: Total, Active, and Completed project counts.

### Editing a Project

1. Click **Edit** on a project card.
2. Update any of: completion % (use the slider or type a value), status, due date, or log/notes.
3. Click **Save** to confirm. The last updated timestamp is set automatically.

---

## Edit Member KPIs

This page allows you to update KPI actuals for individual members on your team.

### Updating a Member's KPIs

1. Find the member in the list (shown with avatar, name, title, completion rate, and status).
2. Click to expand their KR list.
3. For each KR, update the **actual** value as needed.
4. Save your changes.

The member's overall completion rate recalculates automatically based on the updated actuals.

---

## Monthly Reports

This page shows company-wide reports published by the Admin.

Each report displays:
- The report period (e.g. May 2026 or Q1 FY2026)
- Published date
- Company-wide completion rate
- Department rankings by completion rate
- Top 3 performing staff
- Staff flagged as needing improvement (below 60%)

You cannot create or delete reports — this is managed by the Admin.

---

## Signing Out

Click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the sidebar. This clears your session and returns you to the login screen.
