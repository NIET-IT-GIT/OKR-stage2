Getting Started

Overview

As a Manager, you are responsible for reviewing the check-in submissions from your direct team members and deciding whether their reported actual values are accurate. Your approval determines what gets counted in their KR completion rates.

PageWhat you do here
Team OverviewSee all your team members, their completion rates, and pending submission counts
Approve SubmissionsReview and approve or reject check-in submissions from your team
ReportsRead company-wide OKR reports published by the Admin team
Access

Signing In

The portal is accessed through your NIET Group Microsoft account — the same credentials you use for Outlook and Office 365. No separate password is required.

  1. 1
    Open the portal in your browserNavigate to the OKR portal address provided by your admin. The landing page shows a single sign-in button.
  2. 2
    Click "Sign in with Microsoft"You will be redirected to the Microsoft login page.
  3. 3
    Enter your NIET Group credentialsUse your NIET Group email address (e.g. firstname.lastname@niet.edu.au) and your usual Microsoft password.
  4. 4
    You are signed inMicrosoft redirects you back to the portal. As a Manager you will land on the Manager Portal dashboard by default.
✓
Already signed in to Microsoft?If you are already logged in to your NIET Group Microsoft account on the device, clicking "Sign in with Microsoft" will authenticate you instantly without prompting for a password.
⚠
Sign in issues?If you see an error after signing in, your account may not be set up with the Manager role in the portal yet. Contact your admin to confirm your account and role assignment.

Managers have access to two portals:

  • Member Portal — to submit your own personal KR check-ins, just like any other staff member

Team Overview

Viewing Your Team

The Team Overview shows every member in your department with their current KR completion rate and a colour-coded status badge. Use this page to spot who needs attention and who has pending submissions awaiting your review.

Completion Rate
The member's average KR rate across answered and approved check-ins
Status
Green = at or above target pace · Amber = at risk · Red = below threshold
Last submission
Date of the most recent answered check-in from that member
⚠
0% does not always mean poor performanceA member showing 0% may simply have had no check-ins sent to them yet for the current period. Check with your admin whether check-ins have been dispatched before drawing conclusions.
Approvals

Approving a Submission

When a team member submits a check-in response (either Yes or No), it appears in your Approve Submissions queue. A badge on the nav item shows how many are waiting.

Each submission card shows:

  • The member's name and the KR being checked
  • The target value and the review period it covers
  • The member's answer (Yes or No)
  • Their reported actual value
  • Their reason (only shown for No answers)
  1. 1
    Read the submission card carefullyReview the KR, the target, the member's answer, their actual value, and (for No) their reason.
  2. 2
    Click Approve if you agree with their reported actual valueThe member's KR actual is set to the figure they submitted. Their completion rate updates immediately.
✓
Review promptlyMembers' rates show as unchanged until you approve or reject. Timely reviews ensure the leaderboard and company overview reflect current performance accurately.
Approvals

Rejecting a Submission

Reject a submission when you believe the member's reported actual value does not accurately reflect their performance. You are required to provide a corrected value when rejecting.

  1. 1
    Click Reject on the submission cardAn inline form opens — you stay on the same page.
  2. 2
    Enter the correct actual valueType the number you believe accurately reflects the member's performance for that period.
  3. 3
    Confirm the rejectionThe member's KR actual is immediately set to your entered value. Their completion rate recalculates.
⚠
Rejection is finalOnce rejected, the member's KR is updated with the value you provided. There is no further appeal step in the system. If you make an error, contact your admin — only admins can modify approved data.

Reports

Company Reports

The Reports page shows all company-wide OKR reports published by the Admin team. The content is the same as what members see. Reports are read-only for Managers — only Admins can publish, edit, or delete them.

Each published report contains:

  • Company Rate — overall completion rate at time of publishing
  • Department Rankings — each department's KR completion rate
  • Top Performers — highest-achieving staff for that period
  • Action Required — staff who were below target pace
  • Submission Rate — percentage of check-ins that received a "Yes" answer
